# Community conduct

Welcome learners and experienced contributors. Be respectful, specific and patient.
Critique code and claims, not people. Explain uncertainty and allow corrections.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, unwanted sexual attention, publication
of private information and deliberately fabricated evidence are unacceptable.
These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests and discussions.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate contributions, close discussions, warn
participants or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate and
protect affected participants' privacy.

For sensitive concerns, do not post details publicly. If no private contact route
is available through the maintainer's GitHub profile, open a minimal issue asking
for one, without naming people or describing the incident. GitHub's own reporting
and blocking controls are available for platform abuse.

The current maintainer is [dharmendrathinks](https://github.com/dharmendrathinks).
This policy promises neither confidentiality for public posts nor a response SLA.
